We're here to help

DivineTalk is operated by Del Principe Ventures LLC. Contact support@divinetalk.app for help with your account, purchases, community content, or privacy.

Already paying, but a feature is locked?

Sign in with the same DivineTalk account you used before. Open the subscription screen and choose Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If access is still missing, email us your DivineTalk sign-in address and an Apple order reference. Never send your password or full payment-card details.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DivineTalk. Apple manages subscription billing, cancellation, and applicable refund requests. Deleting DivineTalk or your DivineTalk account does not cancel a subscription.

Optional mission support

Mission support purchases are optional, one-time tips. They do not renew or unlock Premium. DivineTalk is a business; these tips are not tax-deductible charitable donations. The price and currency shown by Apple apply to your purchase.

Account and privacy requests

In DivineTalk, open Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account to request account deletion. You may also email support@divinetalk.app for help or to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. We may need to verify ownership before acting on a request.

Community safety

Use the menu on a community post or comment to report a concern or block a member. Manage your block list from Community. You can also email us with a description of the issue; avoid including unnecessary private information about other people.

Daily verse widgets

Open Home → Your Daily Rhythm → Widgets, create or edit a design, then save it. Add DivineTalk's Daily verse widget through your iPhone's Home Screen or Lock Screen widget picker. iOS controls refresh timing; it may take a little time for a saved design to appear.

AI guidance

AI responses may be inaccurate. Compare Scripture references with the Bible and use your own judgment. DivineTalk is not an emergency service or a replacement for qualified medical, legal, mental-health, or pastoral support.
